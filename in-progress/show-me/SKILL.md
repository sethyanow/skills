---
name: show-me
description: Generate an HTML artifact to explain what the agent is saying. Offer when a topic seems confusing to the user.
---

Generate an HTML artifact with some nice visuals and details the user can browse through to explore what you're discussing.

Especially highlight options to open questions with drilldowns, considerations, and possibilities as things open up. Synergies and hybrids are always welcome.

If the user passed arguments, treat them as what to focus on and tailor the content accordingly.
